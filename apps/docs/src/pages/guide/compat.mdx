## Known differences

- {failedPatterns ? <>{failedPatterns} of {int(totalPatterns)} patterns fail to convert.</> : <>All {int(totalPatterns)} patterns convert. One in the <code>ahk2</code> grammar is rewritten first, because oniguruma-to-es reads its <code>\xff</code> as a multibyte sequence; the token checks cover it.</>}
- {int(capMapPatterns)} patterns need a capture map and {int(clipPatterns)} use `\G` away from the start. They convert, and the token checks cover them.
- When a marker-only comment is the last line of the snippet, Shiki leaves a stray newline before `</code>`. Hilite does not.
